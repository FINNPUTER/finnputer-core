WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS

Phantom has a Sign Message screen in its developer settings. Several wallets, Solflare among them, have no such screen at all, so the only way through was a browser console. That is not a step anyone should have to take to prove an address is theirs.

This page runs entirely in your browser. The text and the signature are never sent anywhere. There is no server behind it.
