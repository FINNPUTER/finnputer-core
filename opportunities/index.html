SIGNALS

A signal fires when several wallets CORE rates highly buy the same token inside one window. Each one below shows the arithmetic that produced it: the inputs, the weights, the total. Check it yourself.

HOW A SIGNAL IS BUILT

Three parts, fixed weights, no discretion.

SMART MONEY · 50%

How many watched wallets bought, how highly they score, and how tightly the buys cluster in time. Four wallets inside twenty minutes counts for more than four across two days.

SAFETY · 30%

The scan. Bundled supply, clusters, forwarded supply, fresh wallets, contract flags. A token that fails hard is dropped regardless of who bought it.

MOMENTUM · 20%

Liquidity, and how far into its life the token is. Enough to get out of, early enough to matter.

When a part cannot be measured it is named rather than scored as zero. A signal built on two of three parts is not the same as one built on three, and you can see which you are looking at.

WHY WE PRINT THE METHOD

Every other signal product treats its method as the product and will not describe it. Ours is printed above, in full.

The method is not the moat. The measurements are, and those take months of scanning to accumulate. Anyone can read how the score is built. Nobody else can tell you that the wallet buying this token also caught four runners under sixty thousand, because nobody else was watching the buyers when it happened.
