WHY THIS EXISTS

Every tool on the market checks the token: is the contract clean, is liquidity locked, who holds the top ten. That data is sold by vendors, which is why everyone has it and why it separates nobody.

WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

It does not move your funds. There is no endpoint here that signs, spends or holds a key, and that is a boundary rather than an omission. Buy buttons hand you to the Telegram bot with the trade set up, and nothing happens until you confirm it there.

It does not predict. The score says a wallet was early and closed in profit. Whether that repeats is not something anyone knows.

It does not pretend to complete coverage. A wallet that made a fortune in tokens FINNPUTER never scanned is invisible here, and a score built on thin evidence is marked as such rather than dressed up. All the limits, written down.
