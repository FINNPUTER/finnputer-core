THIS WEBSITE

This site needs no account and sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It reads our own API to display analysis.

Our hosting and API providers process technical connection data such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the page and to defend against abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a working and secure service, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Wallet addresses and token addresses you look up here are public blockchain data. They are not personal data we hold about you, and a lookup is not linked to you.

THE TELEGRAM BOT

If you use our Telegram bot, we store your Telegram user ID, the wallet addresses generated for you, your settings and your trade history. This is needed to provide the service you asked for, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

We do not receive your phone number or your Telegram contacts. Telegram is a separate controller for the messaging layer and has its own privacy policy.

Private keys are held encrypted. Nobody at FINNPUTER can read them out of the interface. This is a custodial arrangement: if you lose access to your Telegram account, we cannot restore your keys for you.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not build profiles of you for marketing.

We do use third party services to operate: hosting, blockchain nodes, price and explorer APIs, and an AI provider for the assistant. They process only what is needed for the request at hand.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Account and trade records are kept while your account exists and afterwards only where a legal retention period applies. Technical connection logs are kept for a short period for security and then deleted.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to partners@finnputerdex.com and we will answer within one month.

You may also complain to a supervisory authority. In Portugal that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).
