THE RECORD

Tokens that ran, and what was recorded while they did. Every number below is measured: the market cap when a scan first saw it, the peak it reached, and how many of its early buyers are now scored.

These are not calls. The detector finds a token after it has run, not before. Anyone claiming otherwise is selling you a screenshot.

What it does prove is the part that carries forward: the wallets that bought these early were being watched at the time, and they are scored and followed now. The next thing they buy shows up in the live feed while it is still happening.
