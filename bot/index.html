USING THE BOT

What every button
actually does.

The bot holds a wallet for you on five chains, scans any address you paste, and can trade on its own while you are away. This page is the whole thing, in order.

FIRST FIVE MINUTES

1. Open it

Start the bot in Telegram. A wallet is created for you on each chain the moment you do. Nobody else is asked and nothing is imported.

2. Send funds to the right address

The main menu shows one address and names the chain it belongs to. The Solana address and the EVM address are different, and sending to the wrong one is the most common first mistake. Switch chain with the SOL / BASE / ETH / RH / STABLE row, then copy the address that appears.

The menu lists every chain and what it holds, so you can see where your money is without switching.

3. Keep gas on the EVM chains

Base, Ethereum, Robinhood and Stable pay gas in ETH, on that chain's own address. A sell is two transactions there, an approve and the swap, so a position can be unsellable while the balance still shows a value. A small amount of ETH per chain is enough.

4. Paste an address

Any token address, in the chat. The bot works out which chain trades it, scans it, and shows you who bought: clusters, funding between the early buyers, fresh wallets, holder concentration, liquidity. Buy buttons are underneath.

READING A SCAN

The safety score is the token: contract flags, holder spread, bundling. It is not a prediction and a high score is not a recommendation.

The line that matters more is the one about buyers. "2 of the first 22 buyers are wallets we score" means somebody with a record is in early. "None of the first 30 buyers has a record with us" means the token passed the checks and nobody we rate has touched it. Both are findings. Neither is a call.

A check that could not run is reported as unchecked, never as a pass. If liquidity could not be read, it says so instead of showing zero.

BUYING AND SELLING

Buy from a scan or from the Buy button. Sell from Positions: pick a percentage or type your own. Slippage is under Settings and applies to every trade.

The fee is 0.8% per trade. Nothing else is charged.

Positions shows what you hold on every chain, the entry where the bot recorded one, and the profit or loss. A position that arrived by transfer rather than through the bot has no recorded entry, and the screen says so rather than inventing one.

TRADING WHILE YOU ARE AWAY

Auto-buy is the whole point of the bot for most people. It buys on signals without you, inside limits you set.

Budget and size

The budget is a hard stop: nothing is bought beyond it, ever. Size per signal is what each trade costs. Both are in dollars. The budget is a cap you set, not your balance.

Which alerts

Three kinds. Wallet buys is one rated wallet buying. Convergence is several agreeing at once, the rarest and strongest. Scanner picks is a token that passed the bar with no rated wallet behind it, and it is off by default: over our own record it was tradeable 28.6% of the time against 35.5% for wallet buys, and produced six of the seven heavy losses.

Minimum safety score and chains

Nothing under the score is bought. Chains you switch off are never bought on. A signal where the holder distribution or the cluster check could not run is never bought automatically, whatever the score says.

The way out

Every automatic buy gets an exit plan, and you choose which.

Ladder: 1.5x, 1.9x, 3x, 5x, 10x, each selling a quarter of what is left, so 23.7% is still running after the last step. Best average over our record. It gives back a fifth of the peak on the way out, because a trailing stop follows the high.

Half at 2x: half out at 2x so the stake is back, the rest runs with a stop 20% under the best price reached.

All at 2x: everything out, nothing left running. A token that goes to 60x pays the same as one that reaches 2x and stops.

Changing the plan applies to new buys. A position already open keeps the plan it was opened with, because two plans on one position would sell it down twice as fast as either describes.

Start smaller than feels interesting. Set a budget you would not mind losing entirely, watch the first few trades one by one, and raise it only after you have seen it behave. This is memecoin trading and most tokens go to zero.

THE REST OF THE MENU

Orders are your open take profits and stops. Selling a position in full clears its plan automatically.

Copy Trade follows a wallet you choose and buys what it buys, inside limits you set.

Alerts is price alerts on tokens you pick. Which chains you receive signals from is under Settings.

Bridge moves funds between the chains the bot supports.

Withdraw sends to any address you own. Pick the chain first: the address format is checked against it, twice.

Wallet exports your private key. It is your wallet and you can take it anywhere. Nobody from FINNPUTER will ever ask you for it.

Tax Export is your trade history as a file.

Earn 30% pays you 30% of the fees from anyone who joins through your link.

My Signal Record is what the signals you were sent actually did. Including the ones that went nowhere.

HOLDING $FINNPUTER

Holders of $50 or more get every signal ten minutes before the channel. That is the only thing holding changes: the same signals, earlier.

WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

It will not tell you what to buy. Every alert says the same sentence: a token passed the scanner's bar at this moment, with the numbers that got it there, and what happens next is not something anyone knows.

It will not hide a bad outcome. Follow-ups are posted as replies to the original message, which is never edited, and the record counts everything.

It cannot make an illiquid position sellable. If a pool is empty, the exchange refuses the trade and the bot says so rather than pretending the number on your screen is reachable.
